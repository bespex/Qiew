## What's new

- **Column widths by hand**: drag the right edge of a heading to make a column wider or narrower; double-click it to fit the column to its content.
- **Fit to the window**: a button shares the window's width out among the columns – narrow ones keep what they need, the wide ones split the rest – and follows when the window changes size.
- **As wide as the content**: another button makes every column as wide as its longest value, measured in the table's font.
- **Wrap long cells**: a button lets long cells break into lines; rows grow with them, everything aligned at the top, and line breaks inside cells show as such. Large files stay quick, and the row at the top stays there when you switch.

Install: `sudo apt install cusq` (be spex package repository), the `.deb` or the AppImage below. The AppImage updates itself.
